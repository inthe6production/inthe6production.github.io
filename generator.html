Common questions

What's the difference between running watts and starting watts?

Running watts are what an appliance uses steadily. Anything with a motor — fridge, freezer, sump pump — needs a short burst of extra power to start, sometimes 3–8× its running watts. Your generator has to survive the biggest burst on top of everything already running, which is exactly what this calculator adds up.

How big a generator do I need for a fridge and a furnace?

Less than most people think. A fridge (150 W running) plus a gas furnace fan (300 W) plus some lights lands around 1,500–2,500 W once starting surges and headroom are included — a mid-size inverter generator handles it easily. Tick the boxes above for your exact list.

Can I run a generator in the garage with the door open?

No — never. Carbon monoxide from a generator can kill in minutes and drifts into the house even with the door open. Run it outside, well away from windows and doors, and keep a CO alarm in the house.
