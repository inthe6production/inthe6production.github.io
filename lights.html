Common questions

How many lights do I need per foot of Christmas tree?

The classic rule of thumb is 100 mini lights for every vertical foot of tree — a 7-foot tree wants about 700 lights for a full, even glow. Prefer a sparser look? Half that still looks good. This planner uses the 100-per-foot standard.

How many clips do I need for my roofline?

Plan on roughly one clip every 8 inches along a C9 string — about 1.5 clips per foot of roofline. For a 40-foot roofline that's around 60 clips. Buy the bag size above your number; clips are cheap and a few always go missing between seasons.

Why add 10% extra length to the roofline?

Strings never run perfectly straight: they dip between clips, turn corners, and have to reach the outlet. Adding 10% slack means the last bulb lands at the far corner instead of dangling two feet short — and a spare partial string covers any burnt-out section.
