How to fill it in

1. Sit down once with all your pill bottles in front of you.
2. For each medicine, write its name and dose in the boxes for the days and times you take it — "Metformin 500 mg" in Morning and Evening, for example.
3. Tick each box as you take that dose. An unticked box at the end of the day tells you something was missed.
4. Take the sheet to pharmacy and doctor appointments — it's the fastest way to show exactly what you're taking.

Important: this planner is an organizing aid only. Always follow the directions on your prescription labels and your pharmacist's advice — never change a dose or a schedule based on a chart.

Common questions

What size paper does the planner print on?

Standard letter paper (8.5 × 11 inches), turned sideways (landscape). Any home printer can do it — in the print window, choose "Landscape" if it doesn't switch on its own.

Should I fill the organizer from the planner, or the planner from the organizer?

Fill the planner first, from the labels on your bottles (or your pharmacist's list). Then load the pill organizer using the planner as your map. If the two ever disagree, the bottle label wins — and it's worth a call to the pharmacy to sort out why.
