Common questions

Are ceramic heaters cheaper to run than oil-filled heaters?

No. Both turn electricity into heat at the same rate: 1,500 W costs 1,500 W whether the heat comes from a ceramic element, a fan heater, or an oil-filled radiator. They differ in feel — ceramic heats fast and moves air, oil-filled is silent and steady — not in cost per hour.

How much does a 1,500 W heater cost to run for a month?

At 13¢/kWh, running 8 hours a day for 30 days uses 360 kWh and costs about $46.80. Run it around the clock and the same heater costs about $140 a month. Use the calculator above with your own hours and rate.

How can I lower my heating bill without freezing?

Seal first: window film, door sweeps and draft stoppers keep the heat you already paid for inside. Then heat the room you're in instead of the whole house, and drop the heater an hour before bed — bedding holds warmth better than air does.
